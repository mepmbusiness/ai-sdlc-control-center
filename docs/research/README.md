# Research notes

Background research behind the Control Center: how to build with AI agents, how product management works, and what our own experiments measured. These notes inform design decisions. No agent reads them at runtime; agents only see their contract and the input artifacts of their stage.

| File | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [ai-agents.md](ai-agents.md) | Building and running AI agents, organized by theme (agent loop, models, memory, skills, multi-agent, verification, specs, context, cost, evals) |
| [product-management.md](product-management.md) | The product development lifecycle, PM competencies, product risk, and how Anthropic runs product work |
| [own-experiments.md](own-experiments.md) | Results of our own comparison: one agent vs. skills vs. a subagent team vs. a cross-vendor squad |

## How these notes grow

- **By theme, not by source.** New material goes into the theme it belongs to. A new theme is created only when nothing fits.
- **Every claim carries its source.** Sources are listed at the top of each file with a short tag, and the tag sits next to the claim it supports.
- **Own words, not copies.** Notes summarize. Direct quotes are short, in quotation marks, and were checked against the original page.
- **Cautions.** When a source gets something wrong, the note says so under "Cautions" so the error is not reused.
- **Unverified claims stay out.** If a claim could not be traced to its original source, it is not recorded.
